Nice to meet you

Hi, I am Angelo

I photograph houses for realtors in Wayne, Oakland and Macomb counties. That is the entire business. No weddings, no senior portraits, no corporate headshots competing for the same weekend.

I started EZ Shots because listing photography in this market had two problems: it was priced like a luxury and it came back slow. An agent would pay $400 and then wait three days while the property sat unlisted. Both of those are fixable, so the whole business is built around fixing them. Flat pricing at $150 and $250, drone included instead of upsold, and galleries that come back the next morning.

The guarantee exists for the same reason. If I am asking an agent to trust their seller's timeline to somebody they found on the internet, the reasonable thing is to carry that risk myself. Half off the first shoot, full refund for 48 hours after any gallery, free if I am ever later than 72 hours.
