Hiring a Real Estate Photographer in Oakland County: What to Ask

You scroll through photographers in Oakland County and they all look fine. Same wide-angle shots, same bright living rooms. But the details underneath the pretty galleries are what actually matter when your listing hits the market.

Are they actually available when you need them?

This seems obvious but agents get burned on it all the time. A photographer might have a great portfolio but slots fill up fast during peak season. Ask how far out they book, and whether they can shoot your specific address. If they are swamped, you might be sitting on a vacant listing waiting three weeks for a photoshoot. Not ideal.

What exactly is in the package?

Packages vary more than you'd think. Some photographers include fifteen photos, others deliver fifty. Some include every room, others charge extra for bathrooms or closets. You need to know what you are paying for before the invoice arrives. Also ask whether drone aerials are included or if that costs extra. In Oakland County, aerials matter for larger properties and anything near the water.

Who handles the editing?

This one catches a lot of agents off guard. Some photographers hand you raw unedited files. Others deliver fully retouched images with bad angles fixed and brightness adjusted. You do not want to be editing listing photos yourself at midnight before your listing goes live. Ask whether editing is included, how long it takes, and whether they fix things like window glare or cluttered countertops or if that costs extra.

When do you actually get the photos?

Turnaround time matters more than most agents realize until they are sitting by the phone waiting for a gallery link. Ask for a specific timeline and get it in writing. Some photographers promise same-day delivery and miss by days. Others quote three days and deliver in twenty-four hours consistently. If the photographer guarantees a deadline and misses it, ask whether there is any recourse, like a refund or discount on the next shoot.

What is the cancellation policy?

A seller might back out the morning of the shoot. The listing might fall through. Life happens. Ask whether you can reschedule without fees and how much notice they need. A photographer who requires forty-eight hours is reasonable. One who charges a cancellation fee for any reason might not be worth the headache.

  • Can they show you recent work in Oakland County specifically, not just their best portfolio shots from three years ago
  • Do they carry liability insurance in case something goes wrong at a high-end home
  • Will they walk through the property first to plan shots, or just show up and start clicking
  • What happens if the weather cooperates on the shoot day, especially for exterior or drone shots
  • Do they have experience photographing the type of property you are listing, whether it is a condo in Troy or a colonial in Birmingham

The right questions save the reshoot

A listing photoshoot is not the place to discover problems after the fact. Asking these questions upfront is not being difficult, it is being professional. The photographer who answers confidently and clearly is the one who will show up on time, deliver on time, and give you photos that actually help your listing sell. And if a photographer gets defensive about any of these questions, that tells you something too.
