Before you write

Answers to the usual questions

How soon can you shoot?

Most listings get on the calendar within 48 hours of the request, often sooner outside of spring. If it is urgent, say so in the message and I will tell you the earliest slot honestly.

Do you need me at the property?

No. A lockbox code, a seller at home, or a listing partner letting me in all work. Send any notes about what to prioritize.

What if the weather is bad?

Interiors happen regardless. If rain or wind blocks the aerials, I return for that portion at no charge or take it off the price.

Do you shoot outside Metro Detroit?

Sometimes. Anything more than about 30 miles outside the area may carry a travel fee, quoted before you commit to anything.
