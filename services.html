01 / Photography

Interior and exterior photos

The core of every package. Between 25 and 45 finished frames depending on the package and the size of the home.

  • Exposure blending. Several frames per room combined so the window keeps its view and the room keeps its light.
  • Straight verticals. Door frames and wall corners stay vertical. No leaning walls, no fisheye bulge.
  • Consistent color. White balance matched room to room so the gallery reads as one home, not twelve.
  • Honest framing. Wide enough to show the space, not so wide that a buyer feels lied to at the showing.
  • Sky replacement on exteriors when Michigan gives us a flat grey day, which it will.

Delivered MLS sized so they upload without resizing, plus full resolution for print and flyers.

02 / Aerial

Drone aerials, included in both packages

Most photographers price aerials as a $100 to $175 add on. Here they are part of the package, because for a lot of listings they are the shot that does the work.

  • The lot. Where the property line actually runs, and how much yard a buyer is getting.
  • The roof. Condition visible up front, which heads off a question that would otherwise show up at inspection.
  • The context. Water, parks, the school two streets over, the highway that is further away than the map suggests.
  • Flown legally. FAA Part 107 certified and insured, with airspace authorization where Metro Detroit controlled airspace requires it.

If weather or airspace stops a flight, I come back for the aerials at no charge or take that portion off the price.

03 / Video

One minute listing video

Included with Listing Pro. Long enough to walk the floor plan, short enough that people finish it.

  • A real walkthrough. Front door to back yard in the order a buyer would actually move through the house.
  • Aerial open and close. The drone establishes the property, the walkthrough delivers the inside.
  • Licensed music, cut to the edit, so nothing gets muted or claimed on social.
  • Two versions. A horizontal cut for the MLS and your site, a vertical cut sized for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.
