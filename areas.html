Why local matters

A photographer who knows the block

Local is not a marketing word here, it changes the photos.

  • Light timing. Which way a street faces decides whether the front of the house is shot at 9am or 4pm. Knowing the area means fewer backlit exteriors.
  • Airspace. DTW, Coleman Young, Willow Run and Oakland County all push controlled airspace over neighborhoods. I know which addresses need authorization filed ahead of time.
  • Context shots. The park, the water, the downtown two blocks over. Buyers moving into an area are buying the area.
  • Short drives. Being 20 minutes from most of these cities is why a next day gallery is realistic instead of aspirational.
