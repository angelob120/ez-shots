Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We want you to be happy with your shoot. This policy explains cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

You may reschedule or cancel a booked shoot up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time at no charge. Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance, or missed appointments where the property is inaccessible, may be subject to a cancellation fee of up to 50% of the shoot price to cover reserved time.

Weather Delays (Drone)

Aerial work depends on safe, legal flying conditions. If weather prevents the drone portion, we will reschedule that portion at no additional charge, or adjust your package and pricing accordingly.

Refunds Before Delivery

If you cancel a paid booking before the shoot takes place and outside the 24-hour window, you are eligible for a full refund. Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days.

After Delivery

Because photography is a custom service performed and delivered specifically for you, payments are generally non-refundable once the edited media has been delivered. If you are unsatisfied, contact us within 3 days of delivery and we will make it right through re-editing or a reshoot at our discretion.

How to Request

To cancel, reschedule, or request a refund, email bigmoneygelo2@gmail.com with your name and shoot date.
