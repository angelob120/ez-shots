Refund and Cancellation Policy

The plain English version lives on the guarantee page. This is the same policy stated formally.

Last updated: September 2026

The short version. Your first shoot is 50% off. Every delivered gallery can be refunded in full within 48 hours. If a gallery arrives more than 72 hours after the shoot, you pay nothing for it.

1. The 48 hour happiness guarantee

Every gallery EZ Shots delivers carries a 48 hour refund window, measured from the time the delivery email is sent. Within that window you may request a full refund of the amount paid for that shoot, for any reason or for no stated reason. Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe and typically appear within 5 to 10 business days. This applies to your first booking and to every booking after it.

To request a refund, reply to the gallery delivery email or write to bigmoneygelo2@gmail.com within the 48 hour window.

2. What happens to the images after a refund

The license to use the images ends when the refund is issued. Refunded images must be removed from the MLS, your website, social accounts and any print materials still in production. A refunded gallery is not a licensed gallery.

3. The 72 hour delivery guarantee

Galleries are delivered in about 24 hours on average. The guaranteed outer limit is 72 hours, measured from the end of the shoot to the time the delivery email is sent. If delivery is later than 72 hours, the shoot is free: any payment already made is refunded in full and you keep the license to the images.

Two things pause that clock, and both are client initiated: a twilight or second visit that you requested, and revisions requested after an initial delivery. Nothing on the photographer side pauses it, including equipment failure, editing backlog or scheduling.

4. First shoot discount

Realtors who have not previously booked EZ Shots receive 50% off their first shoot. The discount applies to one booking per agent and to either package. It does not stack with other promotions and it does not apply retroactively to a completed shoot.

5. Cancellations and rescheduling

You may cancel or reschedule a booked shoot up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time at no charge, and any payment made is refunded in full. Cancellations inside 24 hours, or a scheduled shoot where the property cannot be accessed on arrival, may be subject to a fee of up to 50% of the shoot price to cover the reserved time.

6. Weather and airspace

Aerial photography requires safe and legal flying conditions. High wind, precipitation, poor visibility or a temporary flight restriction may prevent the drone portion of a shoot. Some Metro Detroit addresses fall in controlled airspace and require FAA authorization before a flight; where authorization cannot be obtained, aerials cannot be flown at that address. In any of these cases EZ Shots will either return for the aerial portion at no additional charge or reduce the shoot price by the value of that portion, at your choice.

7. Revisions

Reasonable edits to a delivered gallery, such as an object removal, a re crop or a brightness adjustment, are included at no charge when requested within seven days of delivery. Requests that amount to a new shoot, such as different rooms, a different season or staging that was not present on the day, are quoted as a new booking.

8. Travel

Standard pricing covers Metro Detroit: Wayne, Oakland and Macomb counties. Properties more than approximately 30 miles outside that area may carry a travel fee, which is quoted and agreed before booking.

9. How to reach us

Email bigmoneygelo2@gmail.com with your name, the property address and the shoot date. Refund and cancellation requests are acknowledged within 24 hours.
