FAQ

Questions, answered properly

Booking, pricing, turnaround, drone rules and the guarantee. If something is missing, just ask.

Booking and scheduling

How far out are you usually booked?

Most shoots go on the calendar within 48 hours of the request. Spring and early summer are the tightest stretch in this market, so if you have a listing coming, a heads up a few days early makes it easier.

Do I need to be at the property?

No. A lockbox code or a seller who can let me in is all it takes. Send notes about what to prioritize, what to skip, and anything about the property I should know before I walk in.

How long are you on site?

45 to 60 minutes for Listing Essentials, 75 to 90 minutes for Listing Pro because of the video. Bigger homes take longer and I will say so before the day.

How should the seller prep the house?

I send a prep list when the shoot is booked. The short version: every light on, blinds open, counters and fridge doors clear, cars off the driveway, bins out of the shot, pets and pet bowls put away, toilet seats down.

Can I reschedule?

Up to 24 hours before the start time, at no charge. Inside 24 hours, or if I get there and cannot get in, up to half the shoot price may be kept for the reserved time. Full detail on the refund page.

Pricing and the offer

What does a shoot cost?

$150 for Listing Essentials, which is photos plus drone aerials, and $250 for Listing Pro, which adds the one minute video. Both are flat rates whatever the square footage, and there are no add ons. See the pricing page.

How does the 50% off first shoot work?

Any realtor who has not booked EZ Shots before pays half price on their first shoot. $75 for Essentials, $125 for Pro. No code and nothing to claim, the first shoot buttons on the pricing page are already at that price. One per agent, no minimum after it.

Are drone photos really included?

Yes, in both packages. Most photographers charge $100 to $175 extra for aerials. Here they are part of the price because on a lot of listings they are the shot that does the most work.

Do you charge extra for bigger homes?

No. The price is the price, whatever the square footage. A bigger house takes me longer and that is my problem, not a line on your invoice. There are no add ons and no size tiers, everyone gets the same thing.

How do I pay?

Stripe, through the buy links on the pricing page or an invoice I send you. Card details go to Stripe and never touch this website.

Delivery and files

How fast do I get the gallery?

Average is about 24 hours, which in practice means shot today and in your inbox tomorrow morning. The written promise is 72 hours from the shoot. If a gallery is ever later than that, the shoot is free and you keep the photos.

How many photos do I actually get?

25 to 30 on Essentials, 35 to 45 on Pro. The exact number depends on the home. A 1,300 sq ft ranch does not have 45 frames worth of house in it, and padding a gallery with three angles of the same hallway does not help you sell anything.

What file sizes come in the gallery?

MLS sized JPEGs that upload without resizing, plus full resolution versions for print, flyers and brochures. Video comes as a horizontal cut and a vertical cut for social.

Can I get edits after delivery?

Yes. Small fixes like removing a stray object, a different crop or a brighter room are part of the job, not an upsell. Ask inside the first week.

Who owns the photos?

You get an unlimited license to market that property: MLS, your site, social, print, brokerage materials. I keep copyright and the right to show the work in this portfolio. If a listing is sensitive and you would rather it stay off the site, say so and it does.

The guarantee

What exactly is the happiness guarantee?

48 hours from delivery to tell me you are not happy and get all of your money back on the original card. It applies to your first shoot and to every shoot after it. The guarantee page has the complete terms, and there is no second document beyond it.

Do I have to justify a refund?

No. Say the word inside the window and it goes back. Feedback is welcome but it is not a condition.

What happens to the photos if I refund?

The license ends with the refund, so the images come down from the MLS and anywhere else they were posted.

Drone and legal

Are you licensed to fly?

Yes. FAA Part 107 remote pilot certificate, and commercial liability insurance. Commercial drone work without Part 107 is illegal, and a listing photo is commercial work.

What if the property is near an airport?

Metro Detroit has a lot of controlled airspace between DTW, Coleman Young, Willow Run and Oakland County. Some addresses need an airspace authorization before a flight, which I request ahead of the shoot. A few locations cannot be flown at all, and I will tell you that before you book rather than after.

What stops a flight on the day?

Sustained wind over roughly 20 mph, rain or snow, heavy fog, or a temporary flight restriction. In that case I come back for the aerials at no extra charge, or take the drone portion off the price, whichever you prefer.
