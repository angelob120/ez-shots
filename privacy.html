This Privacy Policy explains how EZ Shots collects and uses your information when you use this website or book a shoot.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email, phone number and property details, when you fill out the contact form or book a shoot. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not collect or store your full payment card details.

How We Use It

We use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and deliver your shoot, process payments, and communicate about your booking. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third parties to operate: Stripe for payments, TidyCal for scheduling calls, and FormSubmit for delivering contact-form messages. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. Third-party embedded services may set their own cookies.
