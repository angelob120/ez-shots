Privacy Policy

What this site collects, which is very little, and what happens to it.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EZ Shots collects and uses your information when you use this website or book a shoot.

Information We Collect

When you book a shoot or use the contact form you give us your name, email address and phone number, the property address and details about it (size, whether it is occupied, how to get in, and any notes), your brokerage, and the time you chose. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How We Use It

To review, confirm and carry out your shoot, to take payment and send refunds, to email you about your booking, and to answer your questions. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who Handles It for Us

Stripe processes payments and refunds. EmailJS and Google Gmail send booking and contact emails. Railway hosts this website and the database the bookings are kept in. TidyCal books phone calls. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy.

How Long We Keep It

Booking records are kept as long as they are needed to run the business and meet tax and accounting obligations, then deleted. Contact form messages are kept in email.

Cookies and Storage

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser remembers your light or dark theme and, while you book, the time you are holding. The admin area uses one sign in cookie for the site owner only.

Your Choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing bigmoneygelo2@gmail.com.
