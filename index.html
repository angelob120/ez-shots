Questions

The things agents ask first

How fast do I actually get the photos?

The average is about 24 hours, which usually means shot today, in your inbox tomorrow morning. The promise on paper is 72 hours. If a gallery is ever later than that, you do not pay for the shoot.

What is the 50% off first shoot?

Any realtor who has not worked with EZ Shots before pays half price on their first booking. Listing Essentials becomes $75 and Listing Pro becomes $125. One per agent, and it applies to whichever package you pick.

How does the refund guarantee work?

You get the gallery, you look at it, and if you are not happy you have 48 hours to say so. Full refund to the original card. That applies to the first shoot and to every shoot after it, so there is never a booking where you are stuck with photos you do not like. Full details here.

Do I need to be at the property?

No. A lockbox code or a seller who can let me in is enough. Send any notes about rooms to prioritize or skip and I will follow them.

Are the drone shots really included?

Yes, in both packages. Weather and airspace permitting, since Part 107 rules and controlled airspace near the Metro Detroit airports are real constraints. If aerials cannot be flown safely or legally that day, I reschedule that portion at no charge or discount the shoot.

What sizes do the files come in?

Every gallery ships MLS sized JPEGs that upload without resizing, plus full resolution versions for print, flyers and social. Video comes as a horizontal cut and a vertical cut.
