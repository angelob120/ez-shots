How it works

What happens after you choose

Two easy ways to get started — pick whichever you prefer.

💳 Buy Now

Ready to book right away.

  1. Pay securely via Stripe. Your card is processed by Stripe — I never see your card details.
  2. Instant confirmation. You'll get an emailed receipt the moment payment clears.
  3. I reach out within 24 hours. I'll email you to lock in a shoot date and confirm the property address and access details.
  4. Shoot day. I photograph the property — interior, exterior, and drone aerials (depending on your package).
  5. Delivery in 24–48 hours. You receive a private gallery of edited, MLS-ready photos and video, sized and ready to upload.

📅 Book a Time to Talk

Have questions first? Let's chat.

  1. Pick a slot. Choose a time on my calendar for a free 10-minute call — no payment needed.
  2. We talk it through. We'll go over your listing, timeline, and which package fits best.
  3. You get a clear quote. I confirm pricing and exactly what's included — no surprises.
  4. Book & schedule. When you're ready, you pay securely and we set the shoot date.
  5. Delivery in 24–48 hours. Edited, MLS-ready photos and video delivered to a private gallery.
