Why it works this way

The risk should sit with me, not with you

A listing photographer is a bet an agent makes with a seller's timeline. If the photos come back flat, or come back late, it is the agent who has to explain it.

So the terms are simple: you pay nothing to book, you pay half the first time, you pay nothing at all for a gallery you are not happy with, and I hand you $20 for the morning it cost you. If I am late past three days, or I never show and never tell you, the shoot is free on top of that. If the work is good, none of it ever gets used. If it is not, you were never trapped.

Nobody has to argue about whether a photo is good enough. If you say you are not happy, you do not pay (or the money goes back, if you already had) and the $20 goes out. That is the whole test.

The fine print

The details, all of them

There is no second document. What is on this page is what applies.

How do I tell you I am not happy?

Press Not happy on your booking page, the link is in every email I send you. Or reply to the email with your photos, or write to bigmoneygelo2@gmail.com. Say the photos missed. You do not have to explain why, although any feedback helps me get better. If you have not paid, nothing is due and no payment reminders come. If you had already paid, Stripe returns the money to the original card, usually inside 5 to 10 business days. The $20 goes out separately by whichever way is easiest for you.

Does this apply to every shoot or only the first one?

Every shoot. The 50% discount is a first booking offer, but not paying for a gallery you are not happy with, and the $20 that comes with it, applies to every single gallery I deliver to you, forever. That is the part that makes it a happiness guarantee instead of a trial.

Why $20 on top of not paying?

Because a refund only puts you back where you started, and a shoot that missed cost you more than money. It cost you a morning, a seller you had to coordinate, and a listing that is now behind. The $20 is not compensation for that, it is an admission that a refund alone is not enough, and it is the reason I would rather get the photos right the first time.

What happens to the photos if I do not pay?

The full resolution files only unlock with the payment, and the photos are not licensed for the listing until it is paid. If you were refunded, the license ends with the refund, so they come down from the MLS and anywhere else they were posted. I am not going to chase anyone over a thumbnail, but a gallery you did not pay for is not a licensed gallery.

What counts as delivery for the 72 hour clock?

The clock starts at the shoot time you booked, not when I get around to shooting, and stops when the email with your photos hits your inbox. If I do not show up, show up late or go quiet, the clock keeps running and once it passes 72 hours the shoot is free. It only stops for me if I contact you before the booked time to cancel or move the shoot. If a seller reschedules mid shoot, or the property is not ready and I have to come back, the clock restarts at the second visit.

Are there exceptions to the 72 hour deadline?

Two, and they are both about you rather than me. If you request twilight photos that require a second evening visit, or if you ask for changes to a delivered gallery, those add time and are not counted as late delivery. Everything else, including my equipment failing, my calendar being full, my editing running long, or me simply not showing up without telling you first, counts as late and the shoot is free.

What about weather and the drone?

Aerials need safe and legal flying conditions. Rain, high wind or a temporary flight restriction can stop a flight, and controlled airspace near the Metro Detroit airports sometimes needs authorization first. If aerials cannot happen on the scheduled day I either come back for that portion at no charge or take the drone portion off the price, your call.

Can I cancel a booked shoot?

Yes. Cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the start time and there is no charge at all. Inside 24 hours, or if I arrive and cannot get into the property, I may invoice up to half the shoot price for the reserved time. Full detail is on the refund and cancellation page.

Is there a catch?

The catch is that I am betting you will book again. Half price and paying you $20 to walk away are cheaper than advertising, and they only work if the photos are actually good. If they are not, the guarantee costs me the shoot and $20 on top, which is exactly the pressure it is supposed to create.
